# Daisy 1.0.7.36 — echo-free transcripts, back-to-back meetings, steadier briefs

## Fixed

- **No more duplicated lines when the meeting plays through your speakers.** Without headphones, the mic re-captures the other side and every remote line used to appear twice — once as "Remote," once wrongly under your name. The echo filter now recognizes this session-wide pattern and removes the mic-side duplicates. Honest note: with speakers, per-person attribution can never be fully reliable — headphones keep the two sides cleanly separated, and Daisy now gives a gentle once-a-day nudge when it notices you're on speakers.
- **Back-to-back meetings record properly again.** When one calendar meeting rolled straight into the next, the second could end up with screenshots but no transcript — the previous meeting's background transcription pass was hogging the engine and knocking over the new session. The hand-off is now clean: the second meeting transcribes from its first second.
- **Screenshots follow your meeting window across monitors.** Screen capture used to shoot one fixed display; if Zoom or Teams lived on your other monitor, all shared-screen context was missed. Daisy now captures the display where the meeting window actually is — and keeps following it if you drag the window elsewhere. (Browser-tab meetings still use the main display.)
- **The meeting prep brief no longer gets stuck loading.** If your summary provider wasn't ready or stopped responding, the "Assembling from your past meetings…" card could spin forever. It now checks the provider first and gives up gracefully after a timeout with a retry, instead of hanging.
- **Dictation with the Standard engine pastes your text again.** After switching the dictation engine to Standard, dictation could show that it was listening but then paste nothing — the transcription was being over-filtered and dropped before it reached the clipboard. Fixed; short and quiet phrases come through now.
- **The model-setup indicator actually looks like it's working.** During the one-time model load the progress bar could sit still and look frozen. It now animates, shows how much has downloaded (e.g. "412 / 626 MB"), and counts up the seconds while the model finishes loading — so you can see it's making progress, not stuck.

## Changed

- **Your open tasks now live in their own list.** Daisy used to tuck tasks under a meeting when it shared an attendee, but that sometimes filed a task under the wrong meeting with the same person. Tasks now sit together in their own "To close" / "Overdue" section instead.
