# Daisy 1.0.7.39 — a more focused Your Day

## Changed

- **Refresh Your Day without refreshing everything else.** `⌘R` now updates your calendar and day-card brief on the Home screen, keeping the action focused on the part of Daisy you asked to refresh.
- **A calmer Home layout.** The stats column now follows the natural width of the heatmap instead of leaving unnecessary empty space.
- **Voice Profile text selects as one continuous piece.** Copying a profile no longer breaks into separate two-line fragments.
