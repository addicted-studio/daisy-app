# Daisy 1.0.7.42 — broader brand-name corrections

## Improved

- **A much broader product-name dictionary for dictation.** Daisy now recognises more software, platforms, hardware, and services — including Apple, iOS, Microsoft, Photoshop, Steam, T-Банк, Сбер, Госуслуги, and Авито — and restores their canonical spelling when they are dictated in Cyrillic.
- **Still deliberately conservative.** Common Russian words that could be mistaken for a brand remain excluded, so Daisy avoids aggressive or surprising replacements.
