# Daisy 1.0.7.46 — token usage, faster launch, honest capture reports

## Added

- Home now shows how many tokens Daisy itself spent this month, per model, with an estimated USD cost for supported Claude and OpenAI models and a day-by-day graph. Local models stay marked as free; a model whose price Daisy does not know says so instead of showing $0.
- Home warns when the disk is nearly full. Below 3 GB free, meetings record transcript-only — no audio — and until now nothing said so.
- Your Voice Profile can now learn from what you said in meetings, not just from dictation. Off by default; only your microphone is used, never the other side. Turning it on also learns from meetings you have already recorded.
- Help → “Save Log Report…” writes a bug report wherever you choose and copies the questions to your clipboard, for anyone whose mail lives in a browser rather than in Mail.

## Improved

- Daisy launches faster: it no longer checks the internet for a transcription model that is already on your Mac.
- The bug report now asks a few specific questions in your app’s language, and includes free disk space — the detail that explains most “it didn’t record” reports.
- The Voice Profile page explains what its word counter counts, so “0 of 300 words” next to a full library of recordings is no longer a mystery.

## Fixed

- A meeting recorded without audio on purpose — because the disk was full, or because you chose “Don’t record audio” — is no longer reported as a failed capture, and no longer blames a Bluetooth headset you were not wearing.
- The token card’s graph and its numbers now cover the same period, and the card says which month that is.
