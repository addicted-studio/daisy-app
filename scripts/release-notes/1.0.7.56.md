# Daisy 1.0.7.56 — mark what matters, and screenshots that take notes

## New

- Mark a moment while you're recording: bind a key in Recording → Shortcuts, press it when something matters, and that timecode lands in the transcript and leads the summary. It grabs a screenshot too, if screen capture is on.
- Turn your screenshots into notes: take a screenshot and Daisy saves it as a note with the image in it. Right after, hold your dictation key and say what it was about — that goes into the same note instead of being pasted. Off by default; Daisy copies the picture and never touches your original.

## Fixed

- Dictation with a custom vocabulary no longer occasionally comes back empty, and a few quiet accuracy losses on biased words are gone — the underlying Whisper engine was updated.
- If macOS stops Daisy from hearing the other side of a meeting mid-call, capture now restarts itself instead of silently recording only your microphone.
- A microphone that stalls mid-recording (sleep/wake, a USB hiccup) is now recovered rather than dropped straight to paused.
- Daisy steps aside while Caramba Switcher or Punto Switcher is running, so two layout fixers can't garble the same word — and it takes over again when you quit theirs.

## Improved

- Long recordings use far less memory while Daisy writes up the transcript — a multi-hour meeting no longer holds the whole recording in memory at once.
