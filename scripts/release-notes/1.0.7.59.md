- **Home is now a real workload dashboard.** One glass period control drives the 7-day, 30-day, 90-day, and yearly views across activity, calls, meeting time, weekday load, writing stats, and AI usage. Heatmaps and token charts adapt their grouping to the selected range, while greetings change calmly with the time of day.
- **Upcoming meetings open a preparation sheet.** Review the meeting details, project, tag, existing brief, and related tasks; add a meeting plan as text or by dropping a TXT, Markdown, PDF, or DOCX file; then start recording from the same surface.
- **Saved meeting plans are reviewed after the summary is ready.** Daisy reports each plan item separately as completed, partial, skipped, or not applicable, with transcript evidence, timestamps, confidence, and practical recommendations instead of an opaque employee score.
- **Optional sensitive-data protection is available for cloud AI.** When enabled, Daisy pseudonymizes detected people, companies, contacts, and links locally before a remote summary request, restores safe placeholders in the result, and keeps secrets and payment-card numbers redacted. The option is off by default and local providers are unchanged.
- **Meeting analytics can respect your actual working day.** Set working hours to see time outside the schedule and a work-versus-personal breakdown without Daisy guessing your routine.
- **Library cleanup is faster.** Swipe to delete, use modifier selection for multiple notes or recordings, then move the selection to another project or delete it in one action. Destructive controls now keep readable contrast.
- **Calendar recordings are easier to recognize.** Recent recordings live below the agenda, and their recording icon now appears on the left in the calendar colour.
- **Cursor summaries now use the API-key connection only.** The experimental Cursor account bridge has been removed, leaving the explicit and predictable API route.
- Added complete Russian localization for the new dashboard, meeting preparation, plan review, bulk actions, working-hours settings, and accessibility descriptions.
