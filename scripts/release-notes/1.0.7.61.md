- **Your Library now shows every recording folder.** Folders with audio but no transcript, folders split into multiple parts, and even empty folders are all visible — and Daisy never deletes a folder during a Library scan anymore. Audio-only folders offer one-click transcription right in place.
- **Recordings that macOS moved to iCloud are handled safely.** If "Optimize Mac Storage" evicted your recording files, Daisy now shows them as "Stored in iCloud" with a Download button instead of misreading them as broken — a bug in earlier versions could make such recordings disappear. Check iCloud.com → Recently Deleted if you lost recordings this way.
- **Changing the recordings folder always asks what to do** with existing recordings: move them, leave them in place, or move them to the Trash — with a second confirmation before anything is deleted.
- **Built-in audio player.** Each recording's card now has an Audio section at the bottom with separate players for your microphone and the other side, plus quick actions to re-transcribe or delete the audio files.
- **Live microphone meter.** Settings → Recording shows a continuous System-Settings-style input level meter instead of an 8-second test, and the Mac output device can now be switched right from Daisy.
- **Optional background-noise reduction** for the microphone track — a gentle on-device gate that quiets steady room noise between phrases. Off by default.
- **Faster, more honest model loading.** Transcription models moved out of ~/Documents (where iCloud could sync and evict them, causing endless load times) into Daisy's own support folder, and model preparation now shows a real percentage instead of a seconds counter.
- **Small polish:** white recording timer, folder renaming for transcript-less recordings, cleaner empty states, and new contact addresses — hello@mydaisy.io and support@mydaisy.io.
