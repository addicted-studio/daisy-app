- **Prompts moved from macOS notifications to the widget.** Questions and alerts now appear as a compact pill next to the floating daisy — visible over any app, no notification permission needed. Click anywhere on the pill to accept; the ✕ with its countdown ring dismisses it.
- **Daisy never stops a recording on its own anymore.** When a meeting looks over — scheduled end passed, or the room's gone quiet — Daisy asks "Stop & save?" instead of stopping on its own. Ignore the pill and the recording simply continues.
- **App launches always ask first.** Opening Discord, Zoom or another call app now shows a "Start recording?" pill instead of silently starting a session — under every auto-record policy.
- **Discard recording.** The widget's menu now has "Discard recording": ends the session and removes its audio, transcript and screenshots without saving — with a confirmation first.
- **Live meter for the other side.** Settings → Recording now shows a continuous level meter for system audio (what Daisy hears from your call), matching the microphone meter.
- **Better hotkeys.** "Paste my last dictation" gets V-based combos (⌃⌥⌘V / ⌃⌥V), and Daisy no longer lets two actions share the same shortcut.
- **The morning brief nudge is a pill too** — click it to open the brief in Daisy.
- **Privacy filter covers meeting-plan analysis.** The pseudonymization layer now also protects names sent to cloud providers during agenda-coverage analysis — the last uncovered path.
- **Small polish:** "Summary" label instead of the sparkles icon in Home and Library, the widget can be dragged again on the latest macOS beta, and dictation that lands nowhere now points at your re-paste hotkey: "Paste the text: ⌃⌥⌘V".
